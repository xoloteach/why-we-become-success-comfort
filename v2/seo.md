# Why You Want Success But Keep Choosing Comfort

## Description draft
You can genuinely want a better life and still choose what feels easiest right now. This Why We Become episode explores immediate rewards, avoidance, environment design, fear of failure, and the small repeated choices that build self-trust. Comfort is not the enemy: the question is whether temporary relief is making your decisions for you.

Think deeper. Live better. Become more.
